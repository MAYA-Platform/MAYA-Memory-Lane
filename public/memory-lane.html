How It Works

Memory Lane stores linked records with fingerprints. This diagram illustrates sealing, optional aggregation, storage and verification; it does not mean every conversation is saved or compacted automatically. Verification compares available records and hashes. Missing predecessor links may go undetected, so a match does not establish complete history, truth, or authenticity; coordinated changes to records and hashes may also go undetected.
